# Logo do projeto

A logo foi criada com a ferramenta integrada **imagegen**, a partir da solicitação de um tornado azul e preto com aparência apocalíptica.

Arquivo: `chrome/assets/logo.png`. Usado no painel, no ícone da extensão e no README. A captura `previa-painel.png` é uma imagem da interface real renderizada em um teste isolado, com dados simulados.

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: square raster logo for a desktop mod-download automation project. Primary request: a blue and black apocalyptic tornado. Create one striking centered tornado emblem: a broad menacing storm crown spiraling down into a sharply tapered funnel, with near-black storm masses and electric cobalt-blue illuminated ridges. Style: polished dark cinematic digital illustration with a bold, instantly readable silhouette suitable for an app icon; simple enough to recognize at small sizes, restrained internal detail, strong contrast. Composition: square, tornado fills about 80 percent of frame with generous clean margins, centered and self-contained, no cropping. Lighting/mood: ominous, powerful, apocalyptic, cold blue energy. Palette: black, midnight navy, royal blue and sparse icy blue highlights; no purple, red or orange. Background: solid near-black midnight navy, no scenery, buildings, people, border or mockup. No text, letters, watermark or wordmark. Deliver a single finished logo, not a sheet of alternatives.
